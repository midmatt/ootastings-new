## 1. Core Brand Concept

**Tagline in use:** "Swirl, Sip & Connect"

**Core idea:** olive oil tasting as a sensory catalyst for human connection — not a product demo, a networking/relationship-building tool. The site repeatedly frames tasting (aroma, texture, origin, character) as a way to spark conversation and break the ice at gatherings, rather than positioning olive oil as the product being sold.

**Positioning statement (paraphrased from site):** the business designs experiential, guided tastings that turn networking and gatherings into something that feels human, warm, and memorable — built around each client's own story, values, and culture rather than a generic one-size-fits-all tasting.

---

## 2. Signature Experience Structure ("Signature Moments")

The current site describes a named, repeatable experience arc — this is a strong reusable concept for the `/tastings` page:

1. **The First Pour** — opening welcome + a sensory "awakening" comparison to kick off the tasting.
2. **The Guided Tasting** — flavor, origin, and storytelling presented together as guests taste.
3. **The Conversation Spark** — curated conversation prompts designed to open up the room.
4. **The Brand Moment** — the host/client's own narrative or mission woven into the experience.
5. **The Collective "Aha"** — the payoff moment where guests feel the connection land.

This 5-beat structure is worth keeping as a real content pillar — it's specific, ownable, and gives you a natural five-card or five-step layout for a tasting-experience page.
